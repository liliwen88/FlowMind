# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. llm-flow-dsl is dedicated to building an inclusive open-source ecosystem where people of all backgrounds feel welcome and valued.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that creates a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination based on any protected characteristic
- Offensive comments related to gender, sexual orientation, race, religion, or disability
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or unwelcome attention
- Publishing private information without consent
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all llm-flow-dsl community spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories and issues
- Pull requests and code reviews
- Discussion forums and chat channels
- Conference talks and events
- Social media when representing the project

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior should be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated. The project team is obligated to maintain the confidentiality of the reporter of any incident.

Violations may result in:

- Warning or temporary ban from community spaces
- Permanent removal from the project community
